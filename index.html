        Three rules it does not break.

        The plan is rules you can read, not a black box. Here is what it will always do.

        A sore area is off limits.
        Mark an injury and every exercise that loads that area is removed. If nothing safe is left for your problem, PosturePilot says so and points you to a clinician instead of guessing.
        Pain steers the load.
        After each workout you rate your pain from 0 to 10. A rise shortens the next session. Several easy sessions in a row let it build slowly.
        Some answers stop the plan.
        Numbness or tingling, pain after an accident, recent surgery, chest pain, or severe pain at night end the questionnaire with advice to see a doctor or physiotherapist first.

        A workout runs like a real session.

        Full-screen, timed and hands-free. You follow the cues; the next exercise starts on its own.

        Straight answers.

        The short version of what people ask before they start.

        Is this medical advice?

        No. It is general exercise guidance. See a doctor or physiotherapist for numbness, pain after an injury, or pain that does not settle.

        How long does a workout take?

        With the default timings, two rounds of six exercises takes about 18 minutes, and one round about nine. You choose the timings before you start.

        What do I need?

        A wall, a sturdy chair, a doorway and a little floor space. A mat helps for the floor exercises.

        Where is my data kept?

        On this device only, in your browser's storage. Clearing site data removes your plan and history.
