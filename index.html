Questions

Is this medical advice?

No. PosturePilot gives general exercise guidance. See a doctor or physiotherapist for persistent or severe pain, numbness, or pain after an injury.

How long is a session?

Usually around 10 to 15 minutes. Your plan page shows the estimate for your routine.

What do I need?

A wall, a sturdy chair and a doorway. A mat is helpful for the floor exercises.

Do I need an account?

No. Your answers and completed sessions are saved only in your own browser.
