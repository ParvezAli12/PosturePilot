How a workout works (and what "hold" means)
  • One workout = rounds. You do every exercise once (round 1), rest, then go through the whole list again (round 2).
  • Each exercise is timed (about 1 minute). A guided timer tells you what to do and when.
  • Hold means move into the position, then stay still and keep breathing normally until the timer says Relax.
  • Relax is a few seconds to let go before the next repeat.
  • Two sides: for exercises done one side at a time, the timer tells you to switch halfway.
  • Rest between exercises is 30 seconds by default. You can change it, and the next exercise starts by itself.
