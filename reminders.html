Reminders

Choose when PosturePilot should nudge you. Changes save automatically.

  • While the site is open (in any tab, or installed as an app) you get a reminder at the chosen time. Allow notifications to also get a pop-up when the tab is in the background.
  • If you open the site later and have missed your workout time, you get one reminder that day.
  • To be reminded even when the site is closed, add the reminder to your calendar. A website cannot wake itself up on its own, but your phone's calendar can.

Daily workout

One reminder at your chosen time on the days you pick. It is skipped once you have trained that day.

Desk breaks

A nudge to stand up and do the two-minute desk break, spread through your working day.

Calendar files use this website's address, so add them from your published site, not from a file on your computer.
